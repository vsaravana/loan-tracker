# Kadan Kanakku: hosting on GitHub Pages (free)

## 1. Put it online (one time, about 10 minutes)
1. Create a free account at github.com.
2. Tap "New repository". Name it `kadan-kanakku`, set it to Public, and create it.
3. Tap "uploading an existing file" and upload all six files from this folder:
   index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, apple-touch-icon.png
4. Open Settings, then Pages. Under "Branch", choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then Save.
5. After a minute your app is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/kadan-kanakku/`.

The code is public, but your loan data is not. It never leaves your phone.

## 2. Install on your iPhone
1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
3. Open it once from the Home Screen while online. After that it works in airplane mode.

## 3. Move your data from claude.ai
1. In the claude.ai version, open settings (top-right icon on Home) and tap "Save backup file" or "Copy backup text".
2. In the installed app, open settings and restore from the file or paste the text.

## 4. Keep it safe for years
- Back up at least once a month. The app reminds you after 30 days.
- Save backups to iCloud Drive in the Files app.
- Before changing or resetting your phone, take a fresh backup.

## Updating the app later
Upload the new index.html to the same repository, and change `VERSION` in sw.js (for example kk-v2).
Open the app online once and it updates. Your data is untouched.
